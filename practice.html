What every exercise record needs

Purpose, voice type or range guidance, musical pattern, key path, tempo, duration, intensity, instructions, contraindications, source authorship, production status and revision history.

From a Telegram voice note to a finished exercise

  1. Transcribe the spoken instruction and detect the played pattern.
  2. Confirm unclear notes, rhythm or teaching intent.
  3. Create structured notation and a production brief.
  4. Render original guide and accompaniment variants.
  5. Review with a vocalist, test inside a session and revise.
  6. Only then move the approved version toward release.

Current availability

The full practice library is not public yet. The categories above define the product architecture; they are not a claim that thousands of exercises have already been completed.
