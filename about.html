ABOUT VOCALISTO

A serious voice practice that feels natural to return to.

Vocalisto is being built with vocalists, for real moments: waking up the voice, preparing for rehearsal or stage, developing technique and returning carefully after heavy use.

Why it exists

Voice work is often scattered across recordings, notes, videos and routines that are hard to repeat consistently. Vocalisto is designed to turn that fragmentation into clear sessions with purpose, context and progress.

Built with practitioners

Katya and Danila are shaping the product together. Exercises, language and workflows are reviewed as musical material—not generated filler. The library will grow through authored recordings, careful production and documented revisions.

One practice, every surface

The planned experience includes phone, tablet, web and additional supported devices with one account, household profiles and synchronized progress. These capabilities are product requirements and are not yet a public-service claim.

Current stage

Vocalisto is in private development. The public website explains the direction; it does not yet provide accounts, subscriptions, voice uploads or a downloadable consumer app.
