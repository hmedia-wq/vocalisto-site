Can I download Vocalisto?

Not yet. Vocalisto is being built and tested privately. A store listing or uploaded internal build does not mean the product is available.

Where is the web portal?

The account portal is under private development. When released, it will use authenticated accounts and separate household profiles; private practice data will never be exposed through the public marketing site.

Is this medical care?

No. Vocalisto is an educational voice-practice product. Stop an exercise if you feel pain or worsening discomfort. Persistent pain, sudden voice change, breathing difficulty or ongoing hoarseness should be discussed with an appropriate licensed clinician.

Report a problem

Email the device/platform, what you were doing, what happened and what you expected. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive recordings by email.
