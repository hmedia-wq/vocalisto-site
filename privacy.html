PRIVACY

Your voice is personal.

Vocalisto is being designed around explicit consent, separate family profiles, data minimization and understandable controls.

CURRENT PUBLIC-WEBSITE NOTICE · UPDATED 8 AUGUST 2026

Current scopeThis notice describes the public website. The consumer application and authenticated portal are not publicly released. Their full privacy policy will be published before any external testing that collects personal data.

Who we are

Vocalisto is developed by HAUS GROUP LLC in New York. Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@vocalisto.app.

Public website

The public site provides product, method, support and educational information. It does not currently offer consumer accounts, subscriptions or voice uploads. The hosting and network providers that deliver the site may process standard technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested page and timestamps for security and reliable delivery.

No voice collection on this website

The public website does not request microphone access and does not accept voice recordings. Future voice features must show the purpose, processing location, retention and controls before recording begins.

Future accounts and families

The planned portal will separate each family member’s vocal profile, practice history and recommendations. Paying for a household will not automatically expose an adult member’s private recordings or sensitive practice details to the household owner. Child profiles will use age-appropriate consent and guardian controls.

Subscriptions and devices

The planned service will support a shared entitlement across supported platforms and up to five active devices by default. Store and payment providers will process purchases under their own terms. Vocalisto will retain only the identifiers and status needed to validate access, prevent duplicate billing and provide support.

Analytics and improvement

No consumer-product analytics claim is made before release. Before analytics are enabled, Vocalisto will define the events, purpose, retention and opt-out behavior. Voice recordings will not be repurposed for model training without separate, explicit consent.

Your choices

Future members must be able to view devices, revoke sessions, manage family profiles, export data and request deletion. These controls are product requirements, not a claim that the portal is already available.

Children

Child and family experiences are still under design and are not publicly available. They will not launch until consent, profile separation, content suitability, retention and applicable age requirements are implemented and reviewed.

Changes

This notice will change as real capabilities enter controlled testing. Material changes will be dated and described before the corresponding processing begins.
