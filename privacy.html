Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Vocalisto does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. Nothing you say into the app leaves your phone.

Who we are

Vocalisto is made by HAUS GROUP LLC, a New York limited liability company. You can reach us any time at support@vocalisto.app.

Your microphone

Some exercises measure your breath length and pitch. To do that, the app needs your microphone, and iOS will ask your permission the first time such an exercise opens. You can say no and keep using everything else.

When the microphone is on, audio is analysed in real time, on your device, and discarded immediately. Vocalisto does not record you, does not save audio files, and does not transmit audio anywhere. There is no server that receives your voice, because there is no server.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your onboarding answers: your goal, your experience level, and how many minutes a day you chose.
  • Your practice streak, total minutes practised, and your best breath length.

This stays in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting Vocalisto deletes all of it. We never see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data. There is nothing to sell.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs.
  • We do not build voice profiles or attempt to identify you by your voice.

Children

Vocalisto is not directed at children under 13, and because we collect nothing, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone of any age.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to access, correct, and delete personal data held about you. In our case the answer is simple: we hold none. Everything Vocalisto knows about you lives on your own device and is removed when you delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, we will say so here in plain language before it does.

Health note

Vocalisto is a practice and wellbeing app. It is not medical care and does not diagnose or treat anything. If your voice hurts, or hoarseness lasts more than two weeks, please see a doctor or a speech-language pathologist.
