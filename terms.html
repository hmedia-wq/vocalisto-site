Terms for controlled testing.

These terms describe access to unreleased Vocalisto builds shared directly with approved testers. They are not public launch terms.

Not publicly availableVocalisto accounts, subscriptions, family features and voice processing remain in private development. Access may be changed or withdrawn while the product is tested.

Who may use a test build

Only a person directly invited by HAUS GROUP LLC may use a controlled test build. Access credentials are personal and may not be published, transferred or shared outside the approved household.

Testing data

Do not submit confidential, irreplaceable or third-party material unless the test instructions expressly permit it. Unreleased features may lose or reset data. The current privacy notice explains the limited public website; a product-specific privacy notice and consent flow are required before testing that collects voice recordings or other new categories of personal data.

Voice and wellness information

Vocal exercises and progress information are educational and are not medical diagnosis or treatment. Stop an exercise that causes pain or unusual discomfort and seek appropriate professional advice.

Ownership and feedback

Vocalisto software, branding and authored content remain owned by their respective rights holders. Testers keep ownership of their own lawful material. Feedback may be used to improve Vocalisto without an obligation to adopt it.

Availability

Private builds are provided for evaluation and may contain defects. Features, supported devices and availability may change before release. Nothing on this page promises an App Store release date, subscription benefit or production service level.

Contact and changes

Questions can be sent to support@vocalisto.app. Material changes will receive a new version date. Final consumer terms require legal review before any public release or paid service.
